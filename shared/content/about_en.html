Artsorakelet is developed by Artsdatabanken in collaboration with Naturalis Biodiversity Center. The app attempts to identify species in images using machine learning.

The recognition model is trained at Naturalis Biodiversity Center, using images that are publicly available on artsobservasjoner.no. When the app is used, the model suggests what the image most closely resembles from the species it has been trained with. This means it can only suggest species that exist in Norwegian nature and have been reported with images. It therefore only recognizes wild species (no humans, domestic animals, garden plants, etc.) and provides answers at species level (and some subspecies).

Images are sent to the server for recognition. Images and user information are not made available to Artsdatabanken or others.

You can read more about Artsorakelet on Artsdatabanken's website. Questions and feedback can be sent to support@artsobservasjoner.no.
